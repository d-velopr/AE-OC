3-Day Digital Fabrication Class

📅 October 9–11, 2026  ·  📍 Diamond Bar, CA  ·  Early Bird $1,295

🕘 Check-in 9:30 AM  ·  Class starts 10:00 AM

From 3D Scanning To Bringing Actual Parts To Life. This hands-on training focuses on the front half of the digital fabrication workflow — capturing real vehicle geometry and turning it into accurate, buildable CAD. You'll work through 3D scanning, reverse engineering, and surfacing using the same processes we use every day in our shop. This is not a theory class — every step is focused on real-world application and execution.

What You'll Learn

3D Scanning
  • Capture vehicle geometry using 3D scanning
  • Scanner setup, coverage & scan quality
  • Clean up and align raw scan data
Reverse Engineering
  • Turn scan meshes into accurate CAD
  • Establish reference geometry from a scan
  • Build dimensionally correct, buildable models
Surfacing
  • Rebuild complex, organic shapes from scan data
  • Surface continuity and clean transitions
  • Panels that follow real vehicle contours
Real-World Workflows
  • Shop-proven process from scan to finished part
  • Working in Fusion 360 end to end
  • Taking a model through to an install-ready component

SCAN → REVERSE ENGINEER → SURFACE → REAL PARTS

What's Included

  • Lunch, snacks & coffee provided all three days
  • Networking with fabricators & enthusiasts

Who Should Attend

  • Installers & fabricators
  • Shop owners
  • Audio professionals
  • Anyone modernizing their workflow

By the end of the class, you'll understand how to take a vehicle from raw scan data to engineered, install-ready components using a structured digital fabrication process.
