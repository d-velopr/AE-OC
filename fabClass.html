Intro / Foundation Session

📅 June 27, 2026  ·  🕙 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM  ·  📍 Huntington Beach, CA  ·  $300

This 5-hour foundation session is designed to give you the foundation needed for the full Digital Fabrication Training program. The goal is to ensure everyone enters the 3-day class prepared, so we can focus on execution rather than software basics.

Topics include

  • Fusion 360 fundamentals
  • Basic CAD workflow
  • Scan-to-design concepts
  • Software setup and organization
  • Digital fabrication workflow overview

3-Day Digital Fabrication Class

📅 July 24–26, 2026  ·  📍 Huntington Beach, CA  ·  $1,295

Modern Vehicle Integration Workflow. This hands-on training teaches a complete digital fabrication workflow for modern vehicle integration using the same processes we use every day in our shop. You'll learn how 3D scanning, CAD design, laser cutting, CNC machining, and 3D printing work together to create accurate, repeatable, and professional vehicle installations. This is not a theory class — every step is focused on real-world application and execution.

What You'll Learn

Scan
  • Capture vehicle data using 3D scanning
  • Prepare scan files for production
Design
  • Design components in Fusion 360
  • Create production-ready parts from scan data
Fabricate
  • Laser cutting workflow
  • CNC machining techniques
  • 3D printing applications
Integrate
  • Aluminum fuse holder bracket
  • Speaker adapters & block-out plates
  • Amplifier rack fabrication
  • Under-seat subwoofer enclosure
  • DSP controller integration

SCAN → DESIGN → PROTOTYPE → FABRICATE → INSTALL

What's Included

  • Special guest appearance — learn from industry pros
  • Lunch & snacks provided
  • Networking with fabricators & enthusiasts

Who Should Attend

  • Installers & fabricators
  • Shop owners
  • Audio professionals
  • Anyone modernizing their workflow

By the end of the class, you'll understand how to take a vehicle from raw scan data to engineered, install-ready components using a structured digital fabrication process.
